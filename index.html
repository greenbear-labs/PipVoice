For grown-ups

Pip listens for words like cars, race, robots and candy, plus a few combos like "race car" and "fire truck". When she hears talking but no known words, she answers with a friendly "tell me more" line. After a quiet spell she suggests a topic, and after three unanswered suggestions she goes to sleep so she doesn't talk to an empty room.
